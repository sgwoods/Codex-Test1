## Event-Timing Decision

The initial StreamB comparison preserves all33Stage35 target IDs and all48
shots, score and every recorded player pose. Its bridge catches709952769 at
6.717s instead of7.517s, using a bullet that originally hit566298320. The real
engine then frees a bullet slot earlier and produces different subsequent
shot/kill times; all original killed IDs are recovered. This is a consequence
of changed legitimate geometry, not evidence of forced hits or changed weapons.
Requiring exact event timing would reject the earlier hit even with all
demonstrated scoring opportunities retained. Lead therefore makes event equality
a reviewed diagnostic while keeping actual target/score/input/physics regression
checks mandatory. Changed timing is not described as exact equality or hidden.
Source-independent input replay and release-to-natural-completion coverage remain.
The initial strict comparison and raw data are retained under
private-artifacts/blue-path-oct13/stream-b-initial-shot-comparison.json.
Stage35's0.666s empty wait is a separate candidate blocker requiring correction;
no event-timing decision waives that failure.
