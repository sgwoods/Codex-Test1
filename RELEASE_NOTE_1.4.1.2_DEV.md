  plus an extra transition. Measured clear-to-spawn cadence is about 4.43 seconds
  in the accepted checks, near the 4.401-second reference target.

  The largest measured blue join decreases from 149.46 to 4.72 pixels; the later
  green join is about 1.02 pixels. These are sampled engineering measurements,
  not proof of exact original-game geometry or every possible route.

  retain 37/33/37 hits and their demonstrated shooting opportunities.

  continues. This bounds late enemy-shot speed and dive pressure.
