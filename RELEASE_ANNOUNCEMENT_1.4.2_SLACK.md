# Slack Announcement: Aurora Galactica 1.4.2

Prepared October 27, 2026. Production release: October 26, 2026.
Announcement copy below; not posted automatically.

---

*Aurora Galactica 1.4.2 is live!*

Thanks to everyone who's been playing and sending feedback. This release brings
some substantial improvements to our little arcade:

- *Your scores, by game.* Signed-in runs now save separately for Aurora Galactica
  and Galaxy Guardians, while existing Aurora scores are preserved.
- *A much stronger Professional pilot.* Try Watch mode or play against the AI
  rival. In our ten-run Aurora test panel, Professional had a median score of
  *240,785*, with nine runs reaching 200,000. Each run was capped at 15 minutes;
  these are test results, not promised scores.
- *Smoother challenge flow.* Shorter result waits, clearer exits and preserved
  shooting opportunities keep the action moving.
- *Better account and replay handling.* Repaired password recovery, corrected
  metadata for new history/replay records, and clearer music-playback status.
  Replays remain stored on your device.

Play: https://sgwoods.github.io/Aurora-Galactica/

Give it a run, try watching the Professional pilot, and tell us what feels better
or still gets in your way. For bugs, the game name, stage, browser and a screenshot
or replay are especially helpful. Please crop out account details.

Galaxy Guardians remains a playable preview, and Expert consistency is still
being tuned. Signed-in scores are account-linked, not automatically cheat-verified.

Thanks for helping us make the next version better!
