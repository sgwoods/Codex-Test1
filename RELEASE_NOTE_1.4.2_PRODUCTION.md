Prepared October 24, 2026. User-approved for publication after final gates.
Consult hosted build-info.json for completed deployment identity.
Gameplay source: beta5, ea779a76, build1218. Current production is1.4.1.

This candidate adds per-game signed-in score storage, password recovery fixes,
stronger Professional play, replay history metadata fixes and smoother challenge
transitions. Existing Aurora scores are preserved; Guardians scores are separate.
Signed-in scores are not automatically proof of cheat-free gameplay.

Expert consistency remains an accepted beta limitation. Guardians remains a
playable preview. No new gameplay tuning is part of production packaging.

The player accepted beta5 play and reported successful password recovery.
Live account score retrieval was confirmed for both games, including a new
Guardians160-point run after reload. Documentation generation, PDF rendering
and all production release gates must pass before publication.

beta1.4.2-beta.5. They share source ea779a76 and build1218.
